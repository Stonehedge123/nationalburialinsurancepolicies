Why waiting costs you money

Most people only think about this after something goes wrong. That's the most expensive moment to start. Apply while you're healthy and more plans are open to you, at the rate for the age you are today. Wait for a diagnosis and the same coverage costs more, or the best plans aren't available at all.

Social Security pays a one-time death benefit of just $255. A funeral costs $8,000 to $10,000. Somebody pays the difference. The only question is whether it's you, ahead of time, or your family, the week after.
